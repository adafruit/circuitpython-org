TinyS3[D] is the TinyS3 you know, re-designed with the Series[D] upgrades: an onboard antenna and a u.FL connector on the same board, switchable from your code, an I²C fuel gauge for accurate battery monitoring & a new ultra low power 1010 NeoPixel RGB LED. It keeps its TinyPICO-compatible footprint.

**Features & Specifications**
- ESP32-S3FN8 Dual 32bit Xtensa LX7 cores @ up to 240Mhz
- RISC-V Ultra Low Power Co-processor
- 2.4GHz Wifi 4 - 802.11b/g/n
- Bluetooth 5, BLE + Mesh
- 8MB QSPI Flash
- 8MB QSPI PSRAM
- **3D high-gain onboard antenna and u.FL connector, switchable in software**
- **I²C battery fuel gauge**
- 700mA 3.3V LDO Regulator
- Ultra low power RGB NeoPixel LED
- **ULTRA LOW Deep Sleep Current**
- USB-C Connector with back-feed protection
- Native USB + USB Serial JTAG
- LiPo Battery Charging
- JST PH connector pads on the back
- 5V Sense Pins
- 17 GPIO
- Only 36.3 x 17.8mm in size
- Compatible with TinyPICO & TinyS2

You can find out more about the TinyS3[D] at [unexpectedmaker.com/tinys3d](https://unexpectedmaker.com/tinys3d)
