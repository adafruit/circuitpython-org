ting is a standalone handheld microphone, more than just a mic, ting acts as a live performance tool, giving you hands-on control over your vocals.

Out of the box the EP-2350 runs a custom build of MicroPython made by Teenage Engineering. This download is for a CircuitPython build that replaces the stock firmware. The same process can be used to flash the stock firmware to get the device back to it's original behavior.

The hardware features:
 - RP2350 microcontroller
 - NAU88L21 audio CODEC
 - Microphone
 - 3.5mm audio line out
 - Accelerometer
 - 3 buttons and a volume knob
 - Handle with two endstop buttons and analog sensing via potentiometer
