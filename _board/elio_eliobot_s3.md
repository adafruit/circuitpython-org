Eliobot is an educational robot designed in France to teach children programming, from graphical blocks to Python. It is built around an ESP32-S3 with 4MB of flash and 2MB of PSRAM, and runs CircuitPython natively.

On board:

- Two DC motors with a DRV8833 driver
- LED matrix eyes behind a clear dome, and a NeoPixel status LED
- Four infrared obstacle sensors
- Five line-following sensors
- A buzzer
- Battery voltage sensing
- Expansion connectors for I2C, SPI and UART add-ons

The [Eliobot Python library](https://github.com/Eliobot/Eliobot-Python-Library) drives the motors, sensors, eyes and buzzer. Children can also program the robot with blocks in [Elioblocs](https://app.eliobot.com), which uploads code over USB or Bluetooth.
