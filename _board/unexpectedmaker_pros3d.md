ProS3[D] is the ProS3 you know, re-designed with the Series[D] upgrades: an onboard antenna and a u.FL connector on the same board, switchable from your code, an I²C fuel gauge for accurate battery monitoring & a new ultra low power 1010 NeoPixel RGB LED. It keeps its USB ESD protection, castellated edges, 16MB of flash and 8MB of PSRAM.

**Features & Specifications**
- ESP32-S3 Dual 32bit Xtensa LX7 cores @ up to 240Mhz
- RISC-V Ultra Low Power Co-processor
- 2.4GHz Wifi 4 - 802.11b/g/n
- Bluetooth 5, BLE + Mesh
- 16MB QSPI Flash
- 8MB QSPI PSRAM
- **3D high-gain onboard antenna and u.FL connector, switchable in software**
- **I²C battery fuel gauge**
- 2x 700mA 3.3V LDO Regulators
- Ultra low power RGB NeoPixel LED
- **ULTRA LOW Deep Sleep Current**
- USB-C Connector with back-feed protection
- **USB ESD protection**
- Native USB + USB Serial JTAG
- **JTAG pins on the header**
- LiPo Battery Charging + MicroBlade connector
- 5V presence detection
- 27 GPIO including castellated headers
- STEMMA QT connector powered by LDO1
- Compatible with TinyPICO, TinyS2 & TinyS3
- 55 x 17.8mm in size
