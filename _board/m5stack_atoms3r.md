The AtomS3R is a 24 x 24 x 12.9 mm programmable controller built on the ESP32-S3-PICO-1-N8R8, with 8 MB of flash and
8 MB of octal PSRAM. It has a 0.85-inch 128 x 128 color IPS screen, a programmable button under the screen, a BMI270
6-axis IMU with a BMM150 3-axis magnetometer, an infrared transmitter, and an RGB LED driver. Power and programming are
over USB Type-C, with one HY2.0-4P Grove port and six GPIOs on the bottom for expansion.

### Features

- ESP32-S3-PICO-1-N8R8, dual-core Xtensa LX7 at 240 MHz
- 8 MB flash, 8 MB octal PSRAM
- 0.85-inch 128 x 128 color IPS LCD
- BMI270 6-axis IMU with a BMM150 magnetometer
- Infrared transmitter
- Programmable button
- HY2.0-4P Grove port and six bottom GPIOs (G5, G6, G7, G8, G38, G39)
