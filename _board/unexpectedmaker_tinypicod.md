TinyPICO[D] is the TinyPICO you know, re-designed with the Series[D] upgrades: an onboard antenna and a u.FL connector on the same board, switchable from your code, an I²C fuel gauge for accurate battery monitoring & a new ultra low power 1010 NeoPixel RGB LED. It keeps the original TinyPICO footprint.

TinyPICO[D] uses the CH9102F USB to serial chip instead of the CP2104. If you are using Windows or macOS, you will need to install the driver to be able to access the TinyPICO[D] via USB.

**Features & Specifications**
- ESP32-PICO-D4 Dual 32bit Xtensa LX6 cores @ up to 240Mhz
- Ultra Low Power Co-processor
- 2.4GHz Wifi 4 - 802.11b/g/n
- Bluetooth Classic + BLE 4.2
- 4MB QSPI Flash
- 4MB QSPI PSRAM
- **3D high-gain onboard antenna and u.FL connector, switchable in software**
- **I²C battery fuel gauge**
- 700mA 3.3V LDO Regulator
- Ultra low power RGB NeoPixel LED
- **ULTRA LOW Deep Sleep Current**
- USB-C Connector
- LiPo Battery Charging
- JST PH connector pads on the back
- 5V Sense Pins
- 14 GPIO
- Only 36.3 x 17.8mm in size

You can find out more about the TinyPICO[D] at [unexpectedmaker.com/tinypicod](https://unexpectedmaker.com/tinypicod)

Getting the firmware onto the ESP32 based board is different to other CircuitPython supporting chips.
You can find out more about how at [CircuitPython with ESP32 quick start](https://learn.adafruit.com/circuitpython-with-esp32-quick-start)
